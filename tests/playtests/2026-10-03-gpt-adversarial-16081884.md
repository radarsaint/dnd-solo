### Harmless actions still stop play as unsupported physical acts

Examples: `strike up a conversation`, `take a shot at convincing him`, wave, rub eyes, push chair back, stand, pace, wander toward the fresco, size someone up, eye the cards, `fire back a reply`, `grapple with the idea`, and own-item handling such as `pocket my own coin` / `grab my purse`.

Some physical acts are genuinely beyond the bounded 6c resolver. Ordinary posture, gesture, own-item handling, and idiomatic speech should not routinely stop play if Kit is meant to feel like a normal DM.

## Deeper verb/object fuzz on current main behavior

A broader template pass found a second router problem: the room router's combat verb list is narrower than the combat parser it delegates to.

These obvious attacks are currently routed as `unsupported_action`, not combat:

- `I punch the dealer.`
- `I kick the dealer.`
- `I slash the dealer with my sword.`
- `I strike the dealer.`
- `I hit the dealer.`
- `I murder the dealer.`
- `I bite the dealer.`
- `I smash the dealer with my mace.`
- `I headbutt the dealer.`
- `I lunge at the dealer.`
- `I bash the dealer with my shield.`
- `I chop at the dealer with my axe.`
- `I cleave the dealer with my axe.`
- `I swing my sword at the dealer.`
- `I fire at the dealer with my bow.`
- `I throw my dagger at the dealer.`

Meanwhile `kit_combat.ATTACK` recognizes these verb forms and `kit_combat.aimed_attack` recognizes the dealer as their target. The mismatch is in `kit_agent.room_intent`, whose local `violent` regex covers only attack/stab/shoot/kill.

This means the router currently has both directions of failure at once: common attacks can be refused, while idioms such as `shoot him a look`, `stab at a guess`, and `kill him with kindness` can become combat. The fix should establish one object-aware attack interpretation rather than maintain a short router verb list beside a larger combat verb list.

The same fuzz pass found the exit rule treating every movement verb plus the word `out` as leaving the room: `step out of the way`, `go out on a limb`, `go out of my way`, `walk out my reasoning`, and even `move out of his reach but stay beside the table` all route as `exit`.

## Assessment

Current Kit is materially better than `9b9d6e70`: the exact prior idiom regressions are fixed, the suite is green, table-talk secrecy works, retries are safe, and a general GPT can operate the bridge.

It does **not yet meet the stronger portability bar** that ordinary player language can be trusted without babysitting. The immediate blockers still reproduced on the newest tested Drive snapshot are:

1. router generalization beyond the exact regression phrases;
2. consequential false routes (attack / exit / tub mutation);
3. host-validator friction and the stake-language false positive.

The next router pass should be semantic/pattern-level, not another blacklist of these exact strings.
