## Router adversarial expansion

The exact Claude/GPT audit phrases are fixed, but nearby ordinary English still breaks.

### Consequential false routes

- `I shoot him a look.` -> unarmed-strike roll (also reproduced through live `prepare`).
- `I shoot the dealer a grin.` -> unarmed-strike roll.
- `I stab at a guess: twenty gold?` -> attack-target clarification.
- `I hit on the dealer.` -> unarmed-strike roll.
- `I cut him off. "No."` -> unarmed-strike roll.
- `I murder him with a look.` -> unarmed-strike roll.
- `I slash him a grin.` -> unarmed-strike roll.
- `I step out of the way.` -> exits through the south door.
- `I go out of my way to compliment the dealer.` -> exits through the south door.
- `I move around the tub.` -> `tip_tub`.

### Harmless actions still stop play as unsupported physical acts

Examples: `strike up a conversation`, `take a shot at convincing him`, wave, rub eyes, push chair back, stand, pace, wander toward the fresco, size someone up, eye the cards, `fire back a reply`, `grapple with the idea`, and own-item handling such as `pocket my own coin` / `grab my purse`.

Some physical acts are genuinely beyond the bounded 6c resolver. Ordinary posture, gesture, own-item handling, and idiomatic speech should not routinely stop play if Kit is meant to feel like a normal DM.

## Card backlogs still reproduce

1. During a live twenty-one hand, `I bet 20 gold and watch the dealer closely. I rolled 20 + 5 = 25.` becomes a social restatement. The round remains at the old 10 gp stake; the new bet and watch do not resolve.
2. The Insight variant resolves the Insight read but silently leaves the hand at the previous 10 gp stake. The bet is dropped.
3. `I set a single copper on the table. Deal me in.` offers the game but neither narrates nor persists the copper as the stake; quick resolution then defaults to 10 gp a side.

## Assessment

Current Kit is materially better than `9b9d6e70`: the exact prior idiom regressions are fixed, the suite is green, table-talk secrecy works, retries are safe, and a general GPT can operate the bridge.

It does **not yet meet the stronger portability bar** that ordinary player language can be trusted without babysitting. The immediate blockers found here are:

1. router generalization beyond the exact regression phrases;
2. consequential false routes (attack / exit / tub mutation);
3. the two existing card-composition bugs plus copper-stake narration;
4. host-validator friction and a stake-language false positive.

The next router pass should be semantic/pattern-level, not another blacklist of these exact strings.
