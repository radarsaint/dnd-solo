**Player:** (OOC) Kit, quick rules question: how would I tell if they're really vampires? What do I roll, and does it take my action?

> Kit: Wisdom (Insight): you are reading people, not the room. Out of combat there's no action to spend; just tell me your character is studying them and I'll call for it. What I won't tell you is whether there's anything to find.

> Kit: Not by itself. An accusation is talk; a fight starts when somebody swings or casts. How they take being called out is up to them, and I'll play them honestly, including if they decide they're insulted.
