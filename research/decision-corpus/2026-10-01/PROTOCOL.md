# Decision extraction: first evidence batch

Authorized by Brendon: “Push what you already did to git then begin” (2026-10-01 UTC). The earlier work was published first in PR #23. This project follows Brendon's extraction brief in the current conversation.

## Target

Recover what Brendon notices, why it matters, what he does about it, and when he chooses differently. Extract judgment and adaptation. Campaign facts and prose style stay separate from personality.

## Evidence rules

Authorship labels: BRENDON, COLLABORATIVE, OTHER_DM, PLAYER, AI_GENERATED, UNKNOWN. Attribution applies to the contribution, not the container. A user-owned file is not proof of authorship. A source's author and a record's AI analyst are separate fields.

Prefer direct corrections; explanations of success/failure; postmortems; design intent; before/after revisions; independent repeated choices; DM procedures; reasoned brainstorming; finished design; player prose; isolated flourishes, in that order. Preserve missing reasoning as null and label every inferred interpretation INFERENCE. Do not change Brendon's criticism into a different argument.

Each decision records context, desired experience, observation, diagnosis, decision, underlying principle, anti-pattern, conditions/limits, evidence, authorship and confidence. Separate observed rejection, requested replacement, implemented replacement and validated preference. Assistant-written preferred responses are unvalidated candidates.

## Four layers

1. Raw corpus: source files remain intact at indexed locators; short unmodified excerpts are preserved with native locators and fidelity limits. Full fetched windows remain an intermediate cache outside the publishable repository.
2. Decision corpus: structured, source-linked records and evidenced preferences.
3. Policy hypotheses: cross-project support, contradictions, exceptions and value conflicts. Current Kit core is a hypothesis, not an answer key.
4. Runtime knowledge: campaign lore/state stays in its campaign. No lore is promoted to a personality rule.
