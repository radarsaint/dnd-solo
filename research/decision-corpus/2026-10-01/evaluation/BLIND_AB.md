# Blind preference probes

These are newly authored DM approaches. They have not been run by Kit and are not held-out tests. Both options aim to be competent. Choose A, B, tie, or neither; a short reason is especially useful.

## AB-01

An optional bidding minigame has just been explained. No stake or action has been committed. The player says they would rather inspect the workshop. Known facts: the seller wants a buyer, the apprentice is worried about missing fuel, and the workshop is open to visitors.

**A.** Let the player enter the workshop. Offer to settle the bidding in one quick check if they still want the item, then have the apprentice bring up the missing fuel while showing the benches.

**B.** Let the player enter the workshop. Leave the bidding unresolved in the background, and have the apprentice's handling of the nearly empty fuel bins introduce the next interaction. Return to bidding only if someone pursues it.

## AB-02

An experienced ambassador has been responding smoothly to difficult questions. The player points out a credible contradiction in her explanation. She knows she has a problem but still has useful information and a defensible interest in delaying the agreement.

**A.** She acknowledges the inconsistency, offers the narrow correction she can defend, and turns the discussion toward what each side can still gain. Keep her composure and let the corrected account expose her interest in delay.

**B.** She starts the practiced reply, stops, and corrects herself. Let a brief lapse in composure show that the objection landed, then have her defend her interest in delay and offer the useful information she still has.

## AB-03

A character who has operated industrial furnaces for years inspects a sealed chamber. The grille draws air inward every few seconds. The repeating draft plainly indicates powered ventilation to someone with that experience; it does not identify the mechanism, its location, or a way through.

**A.** Describe the repeating inward draft and the dust gathering on the grille. Let the player connect it to the character's furnace experience and choose what to investigate.

**B.** Describe the draft and add that the character recognizes powered ventilation from their furnace experience. Leave the mechanism, its location, and how to use the discovery open for investigation.
