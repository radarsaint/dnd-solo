## Rule for future additions

Every new research artifact should answer:

1. What sources support it?
2. Is it observation, local interpretation, cross-source hypothesis, cross-campaign hypothesis, or developmental conclusion?
3. What campaign/format constraints might limit transfer?
4. What evidence could falsify or complicate it?
