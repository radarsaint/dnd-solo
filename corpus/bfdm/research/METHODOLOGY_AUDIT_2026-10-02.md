# Methodology Audit — 2026-10-02

Purpose: audit the BFDM research method against the intended corpus and correct places where the method could overclaim.

## Evidence strength and generalization scope are different axes

The earlier method partly conflated confidence with breadth.

A source can provide very strong evidence for a narrow historical claim without supporting a broad claim about Brendon across campaigns.

Track separately:

**Evidence confidence**
- direct / explicit
- strongly reconstructed
- suggestive
- unresolved

**Claim scope**
- event-specific
- campaign-specific
- format-specific
- era-specific
- cross-campaign candidate
- developmental
- general-current-practice candidate

High confidence must never silently widen scope.

## A live archive is not an omniscient transcript

Discord establishes what is documented in the captured live record.

It may omit:
- voice sessions
- off-platform play
- deleted messages
- private DM coordination
- undocumented improvisation
- prep never written down

Likewise, the latest dated prep revision establishes what was documented in that artifact, not everything the DM may have privately intended.

## Source authority is claim-specific

There is no universal source type that always wins.

- What players saw: delivered/live evidence usually outranks stale prep.
- What was documented before play: dated prep/revision history is stronger.
- Why Brendon says he made a choice: explicit comment or retrospective self-report is direct evidence of that stated reason.
- Whether that stated reason fully explains causality: surrounding records may complicate it.

Define the claim before choosing source authority.

## Retrospective self-report is its own evidence class

Current first-person historical context from Brendon should be preserved as RETROSPECTIVE_SELF_REPORT.

Examples include:
- the interpersonal "dating sim" was emergent
- Roanoke sometimes ran at very large scale
- screen names changed between seasons
- Brendon is also DM radar

Retrospective self-report should not be silently substituted for contemporaneous records, and contemporaneous records should not erase it. Disagreement is research data.

## Identity resolution must be explicit

The corpus spans multiple seasons and changing screen names.

Prefer immutable platform IDs. Store aliases, server nicknames, date ranges, and the basis for each mapping. A matching nickname alone is not enough for attribution in another server.

## Project ownership and authorship remain separate

A campaign can be Brendon's project while individual messages, passages, and delegated work remain authored by collaborators.

Track separately:
- project ownership / authority
- passage/message authorship
- decision authority
- implementation/delegation

## Derived research is not independent evidence

S3 v1/v2/v3, prior dnd-solo research, longitudinal cases, and current syntheses can guide navigation and hypothesis formation.

They must not become self-confirming evidence.

New substantive claims should trace back to primary or attributable evidence:

derived claim -> BCE/equivalent evidence -> BCS source container -> native/portable source

Several analyses using the same underlying source are one evidence lineage, not several independent confirmations.

## Archive absence is not behavioral absence

S3 is much denser than later material.

Frequency comparisons across eras require comparable coverage first. Zero later examples may reflect missing ingestion rather than changed behavior.

## Runtime promotion is a separate curation boundary

The research repository is not Kit's prompt.

No finding becomes live Kit behavior merely because it exists under research/.

Future promotion should remain traceable:

Kit behavior/seed -> curated derived record -> attributable evidence -> source container -> source

Voice/style material is a separate downstream artifact from judgment/personality integration.

## Correct target

The method should optimize for:
1. preservation
2. provenance
3. attribution
4. chronology
5. context/format
6. explicit uncertainty
7. independent source tracing
8. separate confidence and scope
9. developmental comparison
10. delayed curation into Kit

The goal is not to produce a persuasive theory of Brendon quickly. The goal is to build a research process strong enough that a later researcher can challenge a conclusion and reconstruct why it was made.
