# BFDM Research Layer

This directory organizes **derived research about Brendon's D&D work**. It is separate from the source archive itself.

The private corpus should preserve three layers:

1. **Sources** — Discord harvests, Google Drive documents, project files, revision history, comments, attachments, and other primary material.
2. **Evidence records** — attributable excerpts, source containers, chronology, provenance, and decision/event records that can be traced back to sources.
3. **Research products** — interpretations, hypotheses, comparisons, developmental models, and human-readable syntheses.

Do not collapse these layers. A research conclusion is not a source fact.

## Current research goals

The project is broader than reproducing Brendon's prose style or cataloging campaign lore. Research should eventually help Kit become useful in at least two modes:

- **Dungeon Master:** run strong solo or small-table D&D using judgment informed by Brendon's long-term work.
- **Creative collaborator:** help design future campaigns, systems, seasons, encounters, classes, races, worldbuilding, and unusual forms of play.

The archive may support additional uses later. Do not prematurely reduce it to a single training format.

## Core disciplines

- Preserve chronology.
- Preserve authorship and provenance.
- Separate observation from interpretation.
- Preserve campaign format and scale.
- Do not treat evidence density as importance.
- Do not generalize one campaign into a universal Brendon rule without cross-campaign support.
- Distinguish practices that persisted, evolved, were abandoned, or were specific to a format.
- Prefer unresolved causality to invented causality.
- Keep raw sources intact even when good summaries exist.

## Current derived work

Roanoke Season 3 currently has three research passes on separate draft branches:

- `derived/s3-decision-cases-v1` — 20 reconstructed DM decision cases.
- `derived/s3-longitudinal-cases-v2` — longitudinal cases following prep → live signal → intervention → downstream effect.
- `derived/s3-revision-family-v3` — revision-history pass separating prep-driven change, live operational edits, live response, and prepared adaptability.

These are **method-development artifacts**, not a finished model of Brendon.

## Important warning about Roanoke

Roanoke sometimes operated at very large scale, with roughly 30–100 concurrent players, multiple DMs/moderators, asynchronous activity, scheduled events, factions, and many simultaneous scenes. Some observed practices may be excellent solutions to that format and poor evidence for small-table DMing.

Every future research case should preserve enough campaign-context metadata to judge transferability.
