# Provisional Creative-Work Chronology

**Status:** source-anchored skeleton, not a complete campaign history.

Machine-readable companions: `registry/projects.jsonl`, `registry/series.jsonl`, `registry/project_relations.jsonl`, and the identity/server registries under `registry/`. The registry preserves uncertainty explicitly; this Markdown remains the narrative research view.

This chronology exists to stop later research from flattening years of work into one undated "Brendon style."

Important limitation:
**Google Drive creation dates are evidence that a document existed by that date, not necessarily the date a campaign began.**
Live campaign dates should be established from Discord/events/schedules when available.

## 2018 — early Roanoke and format experiments

### Early Roanoke / pre-Season-2 lineage

Two July 2018 Drive documents now provide a strong early anchor:

- `The Rowing Oak` — ID `11QDOazLzTM3Mv0E4hnPyFpY1wyYweeCoG-ZX8eyX6ho`, created 2018-07-08.
- `The rowing oak guide.` — ID `1JqDKaRTEx89rAUcNrtSg4I6ZD7XAHRkFTWqDRRCroHw`, created 2018-07-08.

A preserved guide revision from **2018-07-12** announces:
- play beginning Thursday the 12th;
- the first live voice event Friday the 13th;
- three concurrent events with three DMs;
- up to six players per event;
- Brendon, Kyle Morton, and Rob Hubbard as the DM team;
- open RP between those events;
- that this is “the initial test of this style of gaming for us.”

The date alignment identifies July 12–13, 2018 as the opening/live-event window for this source family.

The early material already contains persistent real-time play, settlement jobs/economy, player governance, custom peoples, multi-DM coordination, and explicit feedback solicitation.

A later `Roanoke Season 2` document describes a “previous incarnation” with the same job/barter structure, strongly linking this material to the game immediately preceding Season 2. Until a source explicitly supplies the formal label, research calls this **early Roanoke / pre-Season-2 (likely Season 1 lineage)** rather than silently renaming it.

See:
- `research/source-leads/early-roanoke-rowing-oak-2018.md`
- `research/longitudinal/roanoke-economy-2018-to-s2.md`
