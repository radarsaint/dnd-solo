# Claims and knowers: repair QA

Base: `kit-claims-knowers` at `bacc210`. Scope: the three reproduced PR #16 findings,
the outdated asset validator, and two failures exposed while checking character
switching and same-turn knowledge. Pricing formulas and price data are unchanged.

## Regressions

- A planned forty-gold ring lie cannot authorize a forty-gold passage toll, including
  when both facts are spoken in one turn. The correct ten-gold toll and the planned
  ring lie can coexist. Exceptions retain their currency and speaker, and silent
  claims grant none. Differently named claims can bind to an authored numeric fact.
- A new drink definition cannot become another drink on a later turn or within one
  decision. Atomic rejection rolls back the entire event batch. First definitions
  survive history eviction and reopening the database; older saves recover them
  from the ledger. Narrated visible details also persist.
- Identity-only character changes clear the old sheet. Reusing the adjudicator
  with another sheet uses the new bonuses; host-supplied overrides still work.
- A successful player-initiated History roll exposes the learned ring claim to the
  decision in that same turn. Preparing it does not commit the discovery.
- The validator accepts the current map/art schema 2 and handout schema 1. It
  checks structure, IDs, paths, counts, DM map privacy, player knowledge gates, and
  reveal rules. Private image binaries are not required for index validation.

   commits the ring lie and its Deception/Insight contest.
3. The dealer states a new cherry-cordial detail. The next packet remembers it;
   attempting to redefine it as ale is rejected without changing world state.

These are integration checks with authored dialogue, not a blind preference test
or evidence that Kit's entertainment quality has passed. An offered ring wager
was rejected during authoring because the implemented table procedure accepts gp
stakes; the corrected walkthrough keeps that boundary.
