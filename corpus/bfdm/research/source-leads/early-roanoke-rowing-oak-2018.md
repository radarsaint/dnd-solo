## Why this is probably the pre-Season-2 Roanoke game

The later document `Roanoke Season 2` (Drive ID `1cwHrxRkHo7GQa4seDt38lR1TO619VHgA4jdFSl-CPMk`) explicitly describes a “previous incarnation of the game” with:
- a job system;
- hour-counting for work;
- barter without gold;
- returning NPCs.

Those features match *The Rowing Oak* material directly.

This is strong continuity evidence that the 2018 Rowing Oak material belongs to the Roanoke lineage immediately preceding Season 2. The surviving source currently located does not itself use the formal label “Season 1,” so the research layer should say **early Roanoke / pre-Season-2 (likely Season 1 lineage)** until a source explicitly closes that naming gap.

## Early format already visible

This source matters because several traits of later Roanoke are present at inception:

- persistent real-time play rather than ordinary session-only time;
- distributed/asynchronous player activity;
- multiple simultaneous DMs;
- concurrent voice events;
- player jobs and production;
- economy as a social system;
- settlement governance decided by players;
- custom setting/races built for the campaign;
- written DM coordination material;
- explicit solicitation of player feedback;
- planned consequences attached to communal choices.

This is historical evidence of the **format's starting architecture**, not proof that every later practice remained unchanged.
