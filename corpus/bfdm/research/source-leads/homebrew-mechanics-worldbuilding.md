### Races for the isakei
- Drive ID: `1NIK4fF4Zc676Q9zEWIN0KYjcirS3bTW4N6iSqYCskEg`
- created: 2026-03-03
- 13 available revisions through 2026-03-21
- every available revision reports Brendon Faulkner as last modifying user

Current design method maps external fictional species to existing D&D races/subraces, then reskins features while keeping mechanics largely unchanged.

Examples in the retrieved window include:
- Asari → Aasimar;
- Yautja → Half-Orc;
- Time Bandits → Lightfoot Halfling;
- Themyscirian → Variant Human;
- Na'vi → Wood Elf;
- Gelfling → Forest Gnome;
- Marooned Grays → Githyanki.

Research value:
This is a **2026 contrast** to earlier bespoke Arcanian race construction. It may help answer whether later Brendon increasingly chooses mechanical reuse/reskinning when bespoke mechanics do not add enough value.

Do not infer that conclusion yet; compare actual design contexts first.
