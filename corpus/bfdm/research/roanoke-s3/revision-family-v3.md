# Roanoke Season 3 — Revision-Family DM Judgment Pass v3

Purpose: distinguish **changes Brendon made because preparation itself needed repair** from **changes caused by live player behavior**. This pass compares the rough draft, week breakdowns, change log, Google Drive revision history, and private Discord harvest. It is designed to keep false causal lessons out of Kit's judgment corpus.

## Central result

The revision family exposes four materially different kinds of change:

1. **Prep-audit changes:** Brendon notices a missing function, lost through-line, or weak tool before players encounter it.
2. **Live operational edits:** changes during play that clarify, correct, or stage delivery without changing story architecture.
3. **Live-response changes:** player behavior creates a new story-bearing need and Brendon materially changes what can happen next.
4. **Prepared adaptability:** slack is deliberately reserved before play so later player history can decide what gets written.

A timestamp is not a motive. A document edited during a session is not automatically evidence of improvisation or player agency.

## Source-of-truth rule

1. **Live Discord** establishes what actually happened.
2. **Dated specialized revision closest before the event** establishes what was already prepared.
3. **Change log** can establish why a prep change was made.
4. **Older aggregate text** remains useful for intent but loses authority when later specialized run material contradicts it.
5. **A post-start revision is not player-driven by default.** Require a meaningful semantic delta and evidence of a live trigger.

## Corrections to the earlier S3 distillation

- **Golden Dawn:** remove the prior unresolved implication that player membership may have been a live adaptation. The W1 pre-play run document already supports player-facing Golden Dawn sponsorship and quests.
- **Week 5:** refine “Brendon progressively decompressed Week 5” to: **Brendon pre-budgeted adaptive slack, then filled that slack from accumulated player history.**
- **Live editing:** never use edit-time alone as a proxy for adaptation. W1 and W3 contain substantial maintenance/operational editing with little story mutation.
- **Prep changes:** self-audits are first-class Brendon evidence. Tatankan recovery, Group Checks, Group Prompts, and combat-density repair show him noticing missing campaign functions before players encounter them.
- **Player-driven changes:** Kvatch is the cleanest revision-family example so far: player pursuit precedes a substantive new branch by minutes, and the new branch is delivered immediately afterward.

## Training implication

A revision-aware decision loop supported by this pass is:

**Audit prep for missing functions → reserve slack where future history will be more informative → run the stable spine → watch for repeated or high-investment player signals → make the smallest semantic mutation that supports the signal → preserve consequences → rewrite larger architecture only when local mutation cannot protect the intended experience.**

Every future revision-derived case should retain a causal label. **UNRESOLVED is preferred to invented causality.**
