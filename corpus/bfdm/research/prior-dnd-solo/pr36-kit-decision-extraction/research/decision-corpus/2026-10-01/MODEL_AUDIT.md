## Further current rules requiring qualification

- **Details are invitations:** K07 is a direct counterweight to treating every detail question as permission to expand. N05 removes precision when it attracts attention to an unimportant detail.
- **Boldest entertaining line wins:** N03/N04 show that the purpose of the performance matters. More impressive is not invariably preferred; an NPC's vulnerability can improve the scene.
- **Unconditional preparation protection / unconditional preparation abandonment:** neither is recovered. R01 is a real attributed comment about discoverable constraints, not a reason to generalize Roanoke's structure into all Kit games.
- **Novel interiority:** N01/N03/N06 concern authored characters. Transfer the reasoning to NPCs and external interaction; do not copy narration of the protagonist's feelings onto the player's character.

## Proposed updates for testing, not installation

1. **Conditional depth:** when an activity contains a meaningful interaction, preserve enough procedure to play it. When the player leaves it, shift attention without silently resolving their choices. Test K02 and K03 together.
2. **Perceptible purpose:** evaluate whether the player can perceive a reason to act, a pressure, a relationship or an opportunity. A populated room and valid mechanics do not establish that.
3. **Continuing embodiment:** after a consequential observable move, consider which actor notices and whether their response changes. Preserve each actor's knowledge and established identity.
4. **Purposeful performance:** expand where anticipation, characterization or payoff earns the space. Keep simple answers direct and inhibit jokes whose premise contradicts the scene.
5. **Unresolved preparation conflict:** replace claims of historical certainty with an explicit research question. Collect matched examples of accepted bypasses and defended boundaries before choosing a universal inhibition rule.

These are analyst inferences with source support of varying strength. Brendon has not approved the proposed updates or selected blind preferences.
